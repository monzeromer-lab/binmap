A source-aware binary analysis tool for Rust

Why is this wrong, why is this slow, why is this so big.

Binmap reads your source and your compiled binary at the same time, and uses that dual view to explain why your program is wrong, why it is slow, and why it is large — then proposes changes and proves they work.

Nine views in one window

Target, Size, Tune, Failure, Perf, Replay, Load, Agent and Environment, with a Findings Inspector that stays on screen beside all of them — because a claim and its evidence belong on the same screen.

Every session exports its evidence

The exported artifact is the same data the Findings Inspector shows on screen: every claim, the tool that produced it, and a content digest of that tool's output. A finding is never constructed without at least one evidence item.

Deterministic engine, optional model layer

The engine computes every fact. The model may only cite them.

Findings carry a provenance chain, and no claim is shown without the tool output that supports it. A model claim is capped at Probable and lists the evidence it cites. Running with no reasoner is a first-class setting, not a degraded mode, and the whole test suite runs that way.

binmap
Desktop application · artifact binmap.json · config binmap.toml No accounts, no server, no telemetry.

Requires Linux x86-64, a Rust toolchain, and bloaty and hyperfine on PATH — the app drives them and the Environment panel reports which are missing. perf is optional for profiling, rr for replay.
